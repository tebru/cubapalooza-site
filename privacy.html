Privacy Policy

Effective November 22, 2026

Cubapalooza does not collect your data. The developer has no servers, runs no analytics, shows no ads, and includes no third-party code that tracks you.

What the app stores

The playgroups, sessions, drafts, match results, cubes, players, and archetypes you enter are saved on your device. If you are signed in to iCloud, they are also saved in your own iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit, so they sync between your devices. The developer cannot see data stored in your iCloud account.

Sharing

When you invite people to a playgroup or a session, the people you invite can see and change its data. Apple's iCloud sharing shows participants each other's names as they appear in iCloud. You can stop sharing or remove people at any time, and members can leave.

Notifications and Live Activities

Round timer alerts are scheduled on your device. iCloud may send silent notifications so the app learns about changes made on another device. Neither contains personal data from the developer.

Deleting your data

Delete a playgroup in the app, or delete the app. To remove its iCloud data, go to Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Account Storage › Cubapalooza.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
