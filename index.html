Cubapalooza

Run cube nights: drafts, pairings, and standings for your playgroup.

Getting help

Found a bug or have a question? Open an issue and include your iPhone model, iOS version, and the app version (shown in the app under About Cubapalooza).

Common questions

Do I need an account? No. Cubapalooza stores everything on your iPhone and in your own iCloud. Sign in to iCloud in Settings to sync between devices and to share a playgroup.

How do friends join my playgroup? Open the playgroup menu, choose Playgroup Settings, and invite people. Anyone you invite can add and change sessions, cubes, and players. You can remove people at any time.

My changes aren't showing on another phone. Check that both phones are signed in to iCloud and online, then pull down to refresh the session list.

How do I delete my data? Delete a playgroup from Playgroup Settings, or delete the app. To remove its iCloud data as well, go to Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Account Storage › Cubapalooza.
